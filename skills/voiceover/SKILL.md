# Voiceover

Create narration text and interpret voice instructions from the user's prompt. Determine language and requested voice qualities (gender presentation, age impression, pitch, pace, accent and tone). If unspecified, choose a natural voice suitable for the content. Use only voice models/options currently available through the configured provider; never invent voice IDs. Ask for confirmation if the choice is ambiguous or generation has a material cost.
